Insight

The 2024 launch of Arccos Golf’s Link Pro marked a significant evolution in wearable technology for golfers. This next-generation device allows players to track all their shots and on-course data without the need for a phone. The campaign surrounding its release required a fully integrated approach, covering every aspect of the product’s public debut, from hardware and packaging to digital platforms and multimedia storytelling.
